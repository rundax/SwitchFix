SwitchFix for Mac

Quick & Easy Setup Guide

Fix mistyped keyboard layouts automatically. Follow these 3 simple steps to get SwitchFix up and running in less than a minute.

Setup: 0 of 2 permissions granted
💡 The Universal Principle (Works on Any macOS Version)

Apple changes the look of Mac settings from time to time. Don’t worry! The rule is always the same:

  • 1
    Look for the Blue Icon: Find the blue square labeled SwitchFix with the letters Ab / Aб.
  • 2
    Turn the Switch from Gray to Blue: Gray means sleeping (OFF). Blue or green means awake (ON). Flip it to ON!
  • 3
    Why 2 permissions?
    • Input Monitoring gives SwitchFix "Eyes" (to see when you make a typo).
    • Accessibility gives SwitchFix "Hands" (to erase the typo and type the correct word).
1

Run the Installer in Terminal

This downloads the latest SwitchFix app safely to your Applications folder.

Open Terminal (press Command ⌘ + Space, type Terminal, press Enter), then paste this:

/bin/bash -o pipefail -c 'curl --fail --location --silent --show-error https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rundax/SwitchFix/master/install.sh | /bin/bash'
💡
Helpful Tip: After pasting the command and pressing Enter ↵, the installer downloads, verifies, and installs SwitchFix automatically. Once complete, SwitchFix will open to guide you through permissions.
2

Give SwitchFix "Eyes" (Input Monitoring)

This lets SwitchFix notice when you accidentally type in the wrong language.

In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Input Monitoring, flip the switch next to SwitchFix to Blue (ON).

💡
If macOS asks to "Quit & Reopen": Click Quit & Reopen. This allows SwitchFix to apply its new input monitoring permissions immediately.
3

Give SwitchFix "Hands" (Accessibility)

This allows SwitchFix to delete the mistyped letters and type the right ones.

In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, flip the switch next to SwitchFix to Blue (ON).

🔒
If macOS asks for your Password or Touch ID: This is standard macOS security when changing system settings. Simply authenticate with Touch ID or enter your Mac password.
4

All Done! Look for the Menu Bar Icon

SwitchFix is now running quietly in the background.

💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about permissions and settings.

❓ What if I don't see SwitchFix in the list?

Look at the bottom of the list for a small plus sign (+). Click +, then go to your Applications folder, click SwitchFix, and click Open. Now flip its switch to Blue!

🔄 What if the switch is already ON, but SwitchFix says "Not allowed"?

This can happen after an update. In the SwitchFix window, click Reset Permissions, or in System Settings, click SwitchFix, click the minus sign (−) to remove it, and add it back with (+).

↩️ What if it corrects something I didn't want?

Just press Command ⌘ + Z (Undo) within 5 seconds, or tap the Caps Lock key to instantly restore what you originally typed.

🔒 Does SwitchFix save my passwords?

Never! SwitchFix automatically turns itself completely off in password fields, code editors, and browsers when typing sensitive information. It runs 100% locally on your Mac with no internet required.
